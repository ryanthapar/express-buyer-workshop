Before you spend hours showing properties

You need to know three things: Are they qualified? Are they serious? Are they already working with another agent?

Then comes the big question: how do you get them to commit to working with you?

This is for you if…

  • You're a licensed agent getting online or open house buyer leads
  • You show homes first and ask for commitment later, or never
  • You want a clear, repeatable process from first call to signed agreement
  • You're newer and want to build the right buyer habits from the start

This is not for you if…

  • You're looking for ways to generate more leads (this is about converting the buyers you already have)
  • You're not willing to ask buyers to sign a Buyer Representation Agreement
