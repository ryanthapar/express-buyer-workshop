Before you spend hours showing properties

You need to know three things: Are they qualified? Are they serious? Are they already working with another agent?

Then comes the big question: how do you get them to commit to working with you?

The 15 objections you'll learn to handle

The things buyers say that stall a signed agreement. You'll get a clear, word-for-word response to each one.

  1. “I can find all the homes online. Why do I need an agent?”
  2. “I want to work directly with the listing agent.”
  3. “I just go to open houses.”
  4. “I just look for for-sale signs and call the listing agent.”
  5. “I use online search platforms like Realtor.ca.”
  6. “I'll get a better deal if I go with the listing agent.”
  7. “I don't want to sign anything.”
  8. “I want to look at homes first.”
  9. “I'm not ready to buy yet. I'm just looking.”
  10. “I need to talk to my partner or family first.”
  11. “I'm already working with another agent.”
  12. “Why should I pay you? What's your commission and who pays it?”
  13. “I'll get pre-approved first, then call you.”
  14. “My friend or relative is an agent.”
  15. “Can't we sign once we find a house we like?”
