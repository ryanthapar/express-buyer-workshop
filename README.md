- Set the two button URLs in the `LINKS` object at the bottom of `index.html` (`enroll`, `call`).
- Confirm with your brokerage/compliance: the "#1 Realtor in Etobicoke" years (2018 and 2024-25 are not listed), the "#1 Agent" testimonial titles, and the "over 1,000 buyers" wording.
- Confirm the content assumptions: bonuses are included in the $599, and the live-presentation bonus date is "shared with registrants".
